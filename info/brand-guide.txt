# Brand Guidelines for "Anna UX School" Tracker

## 1. Color Palette (Strictly use these HEX codes)
- **Primary (Indigo):** #4F46E5 (Use for main buttons, active states, primary icons)
- **Accent (Coral):** #F97316 (Use for alerts, overdue payments, urgent notifications)
- **Background (Light Gray):** #F9FAFB (Main app background)
- **Surface (White):** #FFFFFF (Cards, modals, input fields)
- **Text Main (Graphite):** #111827 (Headings, main text)
- **Text Muted (Gray):** #6B7280 (Subtitles, secondary info, placeholders)
- **Success (Green):** #10B981 (Completed tasks, paid invoices)

## 2. Typography
- **Font Family:** 'Inter', sans-serif (Import from Google Fonts)
- **Headings:** Inter Bold (Weight 700), clean and modern.
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular (Weight 400), 16px base size.
- **Small Text:** Inter Medium (Weight 500), 14px (for tags and statuses).

## 3. UI/UX Style & Mood
- **Vibe:** Modern, minimalist, friendly, clean. Inspired by Notion and Linear.
- **Shapes:** Rounded corners everywhere. Use `rounded-xl` for cards and `rounded-lg` for buttons.
- **Shadows:** Very soft and subtle shadows for cards (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`). No heavy drop shadows.
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace (padding and margins). Don't cram elements together.
- **Borders:** Very light borders for inputs and cards (`border-gray-200`).
